After School at Beanlandia

Support after school programs

When the last bell rings, Beanlandia opens its doors. Our after school programs give New Orleans kids a place to build things, learn real skills, and spend the afternoon with adults who take their ideas seriously — and they're free, with no financial barriers for any family.

Your gift goes directly to after school programming at Beanlandia.

Where your gift goes

  • 🤖BeanBots Robotics — the robot kits and parts students use to build and program a REV Robotics Classbot: motors, gears, wiring, and more
  • 🎟️Keeping it free — no cost to families, so every kid who wants a seat can have one
  • 🌱What comes next — BeanBots is our first after school program; your support helps us add more

Want to support everything at Beanlandia instead? Give to Beanlandia or become a member.

Who We Are

The Beaning of Life

We believe there are two powerful paths to a meaningful life: creating culture and being active in your community. Bringing folks together also builds a stronger safety net for our city.

We began as a small walking parade in 2008. During COVID, we became a true force for good — creating innovative efforts that supported thousands of New Orleanians, generating over a million dollars in jobs for musicians and culture bearers, and sparking joy throughout the city when the city needed it most. Who knew all that could happen from a bean parade?!?

After that experience, we wanted to create something long-term, something that would help us continue to "love" on New Orleans. In November 2021, we embarked on building a community space, Beanlandia. A vibrant cultural neighborhood anchor that invites us all to pursue a meaningful life together — through programming for children and adults, a stronger community safety net, and the enduring belief that New Orleans is a Team Sport.

The Why

Love Your City

New Orleans is a special place! It's our responsibility to love it back. That means building community and working together to make it better. We exist to build a network of folks who love New Orleans and are willing to do something about it.

We do this by bringing people together to share interests, create culture, and support one another. That process builds social cohesion — which is a key ingredient in fostering a stronger community and building a safety net.

Parade with us on Lundi Gras!

The Krewe of Red Beans parades every Lundi Gras — the Monday before Mardi Gras. It's the heart of everything we do. If you're a member, you can walk with us.

After School at Beanlandia

When the last bell rings, Beanlandia opens its doors. Our after school programs give New Orleans kids a place to build things, learn real skills, and spend the afternoon with adults who take their ideas seriously.

Our first after school program

BeanBots Robotics

The Krewe of Red Beans is excited to launch BeanBots, a casual robotics club for young people interested in building, coding, tinkering, and learning how robots work.

BeanBots is not a competitive robotics team. Instead, it will be a welcoming place for students of all experience levels to explore robotics at their own pace. Together, we’ll build and program a REV Robotics Classbot, learning everything from motors, gears, wiring, and mechanical design to coding, problem-solving, and driving the finished robot.

  • Cost: Free — no financial barriers for anyone
  • When: Wednesdays, 4:30 – 6:30pm
  • How long: An 8-week program, starting Wednesday, September 23
  • Ages: 8 and up
  • Where: Beanlandia — 3300 Royal Street, New Orleans
  • Who: Open to the public, all skill levels

Beanlandia

A home for New Orleans culture

Beanlandia isn't just the home of the Krewe of Red Beans — it's a gathering place for New Orleans culture in all its forms. We open our doors to other organizations, traditions, and communities who share our belief that culture is worth protecting and celebrating.

The Krewe of Mayahuel — a Mexican parade krewe rooted in Día de los Muertos tradition — calls Beanlandia home. Their annual parade is a testament to what New Orleans does best: welcoming, honoring, and celebrating the cultures that make this city unlike any other on earth. It's one of our most favorite things.

This is what New Orleans is a Team Sport looks like in practice.

Become a member of the krewe
